dew_point_2m - a temperature to which the air temperature should be lowered so that the water would starts to condense.
for example the higher the dew point then more water vapor is in the air

precipitation - refers to the total amount of precipitation in a given hour, measured in millimeters
0.5: chill breeze
2-4: raining
<10: heavy rain

shortwave_radiation - promieniowanie słoneczne 

pm2_5 - concentration of fine particles smaller than 2.5 micrometers (soot, combustion dust, long-range dust). 
It penetrates deep into the lungs and is the main smog indicator. 

pm10 - covers particles up to 10 micrometers and is always greater than or equal to PM2.5. 
High spikes are real smog episodes, so we keep them rather than treating them as errors.

nitrogen_dioxide (NO₂, µg/m³): combustion gas from traffic, power plants and industry. 
A marker of traffic pollution. Reacts in sunlight to form ozone and contributes to PM2.5.

ozone (O₃, µg/m³): ground-level ozone formed from NOx and other gases in sunlight. 
Peaks on sunny summer afternoons and drops at night and in city centers (reacts with NOx). 
Values appear rounded (only 235 distinct values).

sulphur_dioxide (SO₂, µg/m³): from burning sulfur-containing coal and oil (power plants, industry, shipping). 
Irritates airways, causes acid rain. Usually low concentrations.

carbon_monoxide (CO, µg/m³): product of incomplete combustion (vehicles, heating). 
Values are much larger in magnitude than other pollutants, so scaling will matter in stage 3.
